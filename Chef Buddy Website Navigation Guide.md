# Chef Buddy Website Navigation Guide

This guide will walk you through accessing, using, and managing recipes on the **Chef Buddy** website.

---

## 1. Access and Authentication

### Step 1: Go to the Website

* Open your web browser and navigate to the official Chef Buddy URL:
    * [https://chef-buddy-rouge.vercel.app/](https://chef-buddy-rouge.vercel.app/)

### Step 2: Login or Sign Up

* Upon reaching the landing page, you will need to authenticate to access the main features.
* You have two options:
    1.  **Login:** Enter your specific **credentials** (username/email and password) 
    ``` Email: oyinkansola_o@yahoo.com Password: 121212```
    2.  **Sign Up:** Choose to **sign up with your own Google account** for quick and easy access.
* Successfully logging in or signing up will take you directly to the **Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Dashboard and Navigation

### Step 3: Explore the Dashboard

* The **Dashboard** serves as the central hub for the application.
* The Dashboard includes: 
    * A button named "Generate your Perfect Reciepe" which takes you to the input area of the chatbot
    * Your ChefBudy Tools ** to see saved recipes**.

### Step 4: Initiate Recipe Generation

* Click the main call-to-action button, labeled: **Generate your perfect recipe**.
* This action will open the **chatbot interface** where you can interact to create a new recipe.

---

## 3. Generating a Recipe

### Step 5: Interact with the Chatbot

* Once the chatbot loads, its purpose is to take your input and generate a suitable recipe.
* **Provide a minimum of one ingredient** to the chatbot in the input field. For example, you can type "chicken" or "broccoli and cheddar cheese."

### Step 6: Recipe Generation and AI Processing

* After submitting your ingredient(s), **wait a couple of minutes** for the system to process the request.
* The recipe is generated using a sophisticated process leveraging:
    * **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: The system uses its own internal data/knowledge base (data it was trained on).
    * **Anthropic API Suggestions**: The core generation is powered by the Anthropic AI model to provide creative and detailed recipe instructions.
* The complete recipe will appear in the chat window once generated.

---

## 4. Saving and Viewing Recipes

### Step 7: Save Your Recipe

* If you like the generated recipe, look for a button near the recipe output to **save** it (e.g., "Save Recipe" or a bookmark icon).
* Click this button to store the recipe under your profile.

### Step 8: View Saved Recipes

* After saving, navigate back to the **Dashboard** (there should be a clear "Go Back to Dashboard" button or a main navigation link).
* On the Dashboard, click the section or link dedicated to **see saved recipes** to review all the recipes you have stored.

---

## 5. Logging Out

### Step 9: Log Out of the Website

* From the **Dashboard**, locate the **Sign Out** button or link (often found near your profile picture or in a navigation menu).
* Click **Sign Out** to securely end your session and return to the main landing/login page.
